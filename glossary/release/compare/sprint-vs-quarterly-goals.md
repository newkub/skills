# Sprint Goals vs Quarterly Goals

## เปรียบเทียบ

| หัวข้อ | Sprint Goals | Quarterly Goals |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Timeframe** | 1-2 weeks | 3 months |
| **Specificity** | Concrete, achievable | Directional, ambitious |
| **Flexibility** | Locked during sprint | Can pivot mid-quarter |
| **Commitment** | Team forecast | Strategic objective |
| **Metrics** | Burndown, velocity | OKRs, outcomes |
| **Tactical vs Strategic** | Tactical execution | Strategic alignment |
| **Review Cadence** | Bi-weekly demo | Quarterly review |
| **Pivot Cost** | Low (next sprint) | High (Q commitment) |
| **Best For** | Iterative delivery | Strategic initiatives |
| **Combination** | Steps toward quarterly | Higher level guidance |

## เมื่อไหร่ใช้อะไร

- **Sprint Goals**: Agile execution, predictable delivery, team focus
- **Quarterly Goals**: Strategic planning, OKRs, business alignment
