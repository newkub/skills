# Walking vs Sitting Standups

## เปรียบเทียบ

| หัวข้อ | Walking Standup | Sitting Standup |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Energy** | Active, engaging | Relaxed, focused |
| **Duration** | Naturally shorter | Can drift longer |
| **Attention** | Eye contact, present | Phone/laptop distraction |
| **Location** | Hallway, outside | Meeting room |
| **Formality** | Casual | Structured |
| **Remote** | Doesn't translate well | Works with video |
| **Health** | Movement break | Sedentary |
| **Problem Solving** | Parking lot items | Can dive deep |
| **Best For** | Co-located, energy | Remote, detailed |
| **Novelty** | Fresh for awhile | Standard default |

## เมื่อไหร่ใช้อะไร

- **Walking**: Office teams, short updates, energy boost
- **Sitting**: Remote teams, complex blockers, focused discussion
