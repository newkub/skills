# SLO vs SLA

## เปรียบเทียบ

| หัวข้อ | SLO (Service Level Objective) | SLA (Service Level Agreement) |
|--------|------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Type** | Internal goal | External contract |
| **Audience** | Engineering team | Customers |
| **Enforceability** | Team commitment | Legal obligation |
| **Consequences** | Internal review | Financial penalties |
| **Stringency** | Can be ambitious | Must be achievable |
| **Metrics** | Availability, latency | Uptime guarantee |
| **Error Budget** | SLO drives budget | SLA is minimum |
| **Communication** | Internal dashboards | Public status page |
| **Negotiation** | Engineering decides | Business/legal involved |
| **Best For** | Reliability culture | Customer contracts |

## เมื่อไหร่ใช้อะไร

- **SLO**: Internal reliability targets, error budgets, engineering focus
- **SLA**: Customer commitments, paid services, legal protection
