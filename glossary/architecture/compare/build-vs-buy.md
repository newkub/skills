# Build vs Buy (Internal Tools vs SaaS)

## เปรียบเทียบ

| หัวข้อ | Build Internal | Buy SaaS |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| **Initial Cost** | High (dev time) | Low (subscription) |
| **Ongoing Cost** | Maintenance team | Per-seat fees |
| **Customization** | Unlimited | Limited to API/config |
| **Time to Value** | Weeks to months | Immediate |
| **Competitive Edge** | Differentiator | Commodity |
| **Integration** | Native | API/webhooks |
| **Security** | Controlled | Vendor assessment |
| **Data Control** | Full ownership | Third-party risk |
| **Feature Pace** | Your roadmap | Vendor roadmap |
| **Support** | Internal team | Vendor SLAs |

## เมื่อไหร่ใช้อะไร

- **Build**: Core differentiator, unique workflow, data sensitivity
- **Buy**: Commodity functions, speed to market, cost efficiency
