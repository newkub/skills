# Early Optimization vs Pragmatism

## เปรียบเทียบ

| หัวข้อ | Early Optimization | Pragmatism |
|--------|--------------------|------------|
| **Timing** | Design phase | As needed |
| **Performance** | Anticipated bottlenecks | Measured bottlenecks |
| **Complexity** | Higher upfront | Iterative |
| **Cost** | Unknown ROI | Targeted investment |
| **Premature** | Often unnecessary | Evidence-based |
| **Refactoring** | Avoided | Embraced |
| **Measurement** | Guessing | Profiling |
| **Delivery** | Delayed | Faster |
| **Best For** | Known constraints | Most software |
| **Famous Quote** | "Root of all evil" | "Make it work, fast, then fast" |

## เมื่อไหร่ใช้อะไร

- **Early Optimization**: Embedded systems, high-frequency trading, known limits
- **Pragmatism**: Web apps, MVPs, most business software
