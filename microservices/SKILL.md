# Microservices

This skill provides best practices and guidelines for designing, developing, and managing a microservices-based architecture. It covers key principles from architectural design to deployment and reliability.

## Rules

- **[Architecture Principles](./rules/1-architecture-principles.md):** Core concepts like the Single-Responsibility Principle and database-per-service pattern.
- **[Communication Patterns](./rules/2-communication-patterns.md):** Guidelines for service-to-service communication, including API Gateways, DTOs, and choosing the right protocols.
- **[Security and Authentication](./rules/3-security-authentication.md):** Best practices for securing microservices, including authorization and authentication strategies.
- **[Deployment and Observability](./rules/4-deployment-observability.md):** Covers containerization, orchestration, health checks, and centralized logging.
- **[Resiliency and Reliability](./rules/5-resiliency-reliability.md):** Patterns to build a robust system, such as Circuit Breaker, Retries, and Idempotency.
- **[Development Practices](./rules/6-development-practices.md):** Emphasizes the importance of maintaining consistency across services.
