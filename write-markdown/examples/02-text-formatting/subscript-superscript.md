## Superscript

````markdown
x<sup>2</sup>
E = mc<sup>2</sup>
10<sup>th</sup>
````

## Subscript

````markdown
H<sub>2</sub>O
CO<sub>2</sub>
x<sub>1</sub>
````

## Combined Usage

````markdown
x<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup>
A<sub>n</sub><sup>m</sup>
````

## Mathematical Formulas

````markdown
E = mc<sup>2</sup>
a<sup>2</sup> + b<sup>2</sup> = c<sup>2</sup>
````

## Chemical Formulas

````markdown
H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>
C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>12</sub>O<sub>6</sub>
````
