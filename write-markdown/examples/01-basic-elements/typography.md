## Text Alignment

````markdown
<p align="center">Centered text</p>
<p align="left">Left aligned</p>
<p align="right">Right aligned</p>
<p align="justify">Justified text that fills the width</p>
````

## Font Styles

````markdown
**Bold text**
*Italic text*
***Bold italic***
~~Strikethrough~~
<u>Underlined</u>
<mark>Highlighted</mark>
````

## Subscript and Superscript

````markdown
H<sub>2</sub>O - Subscript
E=mc<sup>2</sup> - Superscript
````

## Small and Big Text

````markdown
<small>Small text</small>
<big>Bigger text</big>
````
