## Soft Line Break

````markdown
Line one
Line two (new line with single enter)
````

## Hard Line Break

````markdown
Line one  
Line two (two spaces at end)
````

## Using Backslash

````markdown
Line one\
Line two (backslash at end)
````

## Using HTML Break

````markdown
Line one<br>Line two (inline HTML)
````

## Paragraph Break

````markdown
Paragraph one

Paragraph two (blank line between)
````
